# Research: ideas for single-file labyrinth roguelite

**Recommendation (do first):** keep current chest→key→exit loop; add (1) assist toggle + daily challenge reusing seeded RNG, (2) magnet/heal/freeze pickups, (3) pause-safe fixed loop + pointer-event pad. Skip permanent shop (L) and rhythm sync (M) until after playtest. Seeded whole-labyrinth generation already fits Isaac's seeded-run model [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/250900/The_Binding_of_Isaac_Rebirth/), and progress-as-knowledge fits Hades' rogue-like definition [Supergiant FAQ](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-faq).

## Ideas — what / why this game / effort / source

1. **Assist toggle (God Mode)** — Hades offers God Mode that makes you more resilient and tougher still each death [Supergiant FAQ](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-faq). Fits: boss/shooters/bears timers are brutal in 20×20 fog; add toggle (+1 HP or slower enemy ticks). Effort: S.
2. **Difficulty dials (Pact-style)** — Hades adds Hell Mode + Pact of Punishment for stronger challenge [Supergiant FAQ](https://www.supergiantgames.com/blog/hades-faq). Fits: level scaling already exists; expose as `?difficulty=` multipliers on FG radius/enemy speed. Effort: M.
3. **Named seeds + challenge runs** — Isaac lists “4 BILLION Seeded runs” + “20 Challenge runs” + “Over 100 specialized seeds” [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/250900/The_Binding_of_Isaac_Rebirth/). Fits: extend existing `?seed=` / `?seed=daily` with no-bomb, dark-fog (radius 2) challenges. Effort: S.
4. **Daily score race** — Crypt has “permanent and daily challenges” for “highest scores” / “fastest times” [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/247080/Crypt_of_the_NecroDancer/). Fits: daily seed + localStorage best moves/time; zero backend. Effort: S.
5. **Permanent diamond upgrades** — Crypt: “Gather diamonds to unlock permanent upgrades” and go deeper [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/247080/Crypt_of_the_NecroDancer/). Fits: score→+1 bomb/HP on next run; keep tiny (3 tiers) to stay single-file. Effort: M.
6. **Rhythm-quantized turns** — Crypt is “move on the beat to navigate an ever changing dungeon” [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/247080/Crypt_of_the_NecroDancer/) [Brace Yourself](https://braceyourselfgames.com/). Fits: align bear/zombie/shooter/boss ticks to player-step grid instead of wall-clock; combo window becomes beat. Effort: M.
7. **Magnet / Vacuum** — Vampire Survivors: “Magnet stat affects radius” and “Vacuum gathers all Experience Gems” [VS Wiki](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Pickups). Fits: chest/key pause-drop recovery; lake/hole trails drop gems. Effort: S.
8. **Healing drop (Floor Chicken)** — “Restores 30 Health points” [VS Wiki](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Pickups). Fits: bears/zombies drop heart; hospital stays rare. Effort: S.
9. **Freeze / board-clear (Orologion / Rosary)** — Orologion “Freezes all enemies 10s”; Rosary “Destroys all enemies in sight” [VS Wiki](https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Pickups). Fits: rare bomb-adjacent panic button vs boss; no new enemy code. Effort: M.
10. **Between-level power-up economy** — Vampire Survivors: “Gather gold… to buy upgrades” and snowball; “minimalistic gameplay and roguelite elements” [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1794680/Vampire_Survivors/). Fits: score→dash/bomb upgrade after each exit. Defer — L cost for small labyrinth. Effort: L.
11. **Fixed-step loop + pause** — Game Loop pattern: “processes input, updates, renders” and keeps speed constant across hardware [GPP](https://gameprogrammingpatterns.com/game-loop.html). Fits: pause stops update not render; seeded RNG stays deterministic. Effort: S.
12. **Brief purposeful juice** — Apple: “Add motion purposefully… brief and precise” and “make motion optional” [HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion.md). Fits: existing shake/particles; gate by `prefers-reduced-motion`. Effort: S.
13. **Cheap alpha for fog/aim/particles** — `globalAlpha` is 0.0–1.0 for shapes/images pre-draw [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/CanvasRenderingContext2D/globalAlpha). Fits: fog falloff + shooter aim wedges already alpha-based. Effort: S.
14. **Pointer-Event touch pad** — MDN: touch events are low-level; “use pointer events instead” for touch+mouse [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Touch_events). Fits: replace D-pad buttons with pointer + canvas swipe; keep 46px targets. Effort: M.
15. **Achievements with progress + store** — Steam: progress via `IndicateAchievementProgress`, unlock via `SetAchievement`+`StoreStats`, overlay notify [Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/achievements). Fits: localStorage achievements shown as toasts; store at level transitions. Effort: S.
16. **Build order: map→save→monsters→items→levels→playtest** — RogueBasin 15 Steps: map, saving/loading, monsters/time, items, levels, “playtest frequently” [RogueBasin](https://roguebasin.com/index.php?title=How_to_Write_a_Roguelike_in_15_Steps). Fits: fog+seed first, then enemies, then balance. Effort: S.
17. **Contrast floor for glyphs** — W3C requires meaningful graphics ≥3:1 vs adjacent colors [W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html). Fits: compass/enemy/aim icons must test on lake/swamp underlay, not just fog. Effort: S.
